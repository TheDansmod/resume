    \vspace{-7pt}
    \hrule
    \vspace{5pt}
    \noindent {\normalsize \textbf{Causal Discovery in the presence of Latent Confounders}} \hfill May 2025 -- Dec 2025 (QMUL) \\
    \textit{Advisor}: Dr. Anthony Constantinou \hfill \href{https://github.com/TheDansmod/qmul-msc-ai-code-master-thesis/blob/main/dissertation_report.pdf}{dissertation \faExternalLink*} $\vert$ \href{https://github.com/TheDansmod/qmul-msc-ai-code-master-thesis}{code \faExternalLink*}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
        \item \textbf{Modelling}: Recast score-based structure learning over ancestral and bow-free acyclic directed mixed graphs (ADMGs), for linear Gaussian structural causal models with latent confounders, as unconstrained continuous optimisation with no acyclicity penalty.
        \item \textbf{Theory}: Constructed two maps from Euclidean space onto the bow-free and ancestral ADMG classes, computable in \(O(d^2)\) and \(O(d^{2.8})\) time, and proved surjectivity, invariance under positive scaling, and that every open neighbourhood of the origin attains the entire class.
        \item \textbf{Statistical model selection}: Minimised the BIC (maximum-likelihood fits via RICF) with two black-box optimisers: PPO in a one-step environment with cyclically annealed entropy regularisation, and CMA-ES with a margin-maximising regulariser bounded so as to preserve the BIC ordering.
        \item \textbf{Benchmarking}: On the real-world Sachs protein-signalling dataset, the CMA-ES variant achieved the lowest SHD (22 vs. 53), the highest PAG-skeleton \(F_{1}\) (0.58 vs. 0.47) and the shortest runtime (106 s vs. 250 s) against \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06978}{DCD} and \href{https://proceedings.mlr.press/v52/ogarrio16.html}{GFCI}; across synthetic experiments varying graph size, sample size, density and directed-edge fraction, it matched or exceeded DCD.
        \item \textbf{Performance}: Accelerated the RICF estimator 2.7--54\(\times\) over the \href{https://ananke.readthedocs.io/en/latest/}{ananke-causal} reference implementation on 5--30-node graphs through Numba JIT compilation and precomputed covariance.
    \end{itemize}
