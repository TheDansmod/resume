    \vspace{-7pt}
    \hrule
    \vspace{5pt}
    \noindent {\normalsize \textbf{Causal Discovery in the presence of Latent Confounders}} \hfill May 2025 -- Dec 2025 (QMUL) \\
    \textit{Advisor}: Dr. Anthony Constantinou \hfill \href{https://github.com/TheDansmod/qmul-msc-ai-code-master-thesis/blob/main/dissertation_report.pdf}{dissertation \faExternalLink*} $\vert$ \href{https://github.com/TheDansmod/qmul-msc-ai-code-master-thesis}{code \faExternalLink*}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
        \item Performed score-based structure learning over acyclic directed mixed graphs (ADMGs) for linear Gaussian structural causal models with unobserved confounders, restricted to the ancestral and bow-free classes, for which identifiability guarantees hold in the large-sample limit.
        \item Introduced two maps from a high-dimensional Euclidean space onto the bow-free and ancestral ADMG classes, computable in $O(d^2)$ and $O(d^{2.8})$ time, and proved surjectivity, invariance under positive scaling, and that every open neighbourhood of the origin attains the entire class; combinatorial graph search thereby reduces to unconstrained continuous optimisation with no acyclicity penalty.
        \item Minimised the BIC (maximum-likelihood fits via RICF) using two black-box optimisers: PPO in a one-step environment with cyclically annealed entropy regularisation, and CMA-ES with a margin-maximising regulariser whose coefficient was bounded so as not to alter the ordering induced by the BIC.
        \item Benchmarked against \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06978}{DCD} and \href{https://proceedings.mlr.press/v52/ogarrio16.html}{GFCI} across graph size, sample size, density and directed-edge fraction, and on the Sachs protein-signalling network. The CMA-ES variant matched or exceeded DCD on synthetic data; on Sachs it attained the lowest SHD (22 vs.\ 53), the highest PAG-skeleton $F_1$ (0.58 vs.\ 0.47) and the shortest runtime (106\,s vs.\ 250\,s).
        \item Re-engineered the RICF estimator (Numba JIT compilation, precomputed covariance) for a 2.7--54$\times$ speed-up over the \href{https://ananke.readthedocs.io/en/latest/}{ananke-causal} reference implementation across 5--30-node graphs.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{-7pt}
    \hrule
    \vspace{5pt}
    \noindent {\normalsize \textbf{Application of Actor-Critic Methods to Improving Video Quality Experience}} \hfill Jan 2022 -- May 2022 (BITS) \\
    \textit{Advisor}: Dr. Paresh Saxena \hfill \href{https://github.com/TheDansmod/improving-video-quality-experience-using-actor-critic/releases/download/docs/presentation_condensed.pdf}{report \faExternalLink*}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
        \item Adaptive Bitrate (ABR) algorithms in streaming video players must balance bandwidth efficiency and user Quality of Experience (QoE).
        \item Investigated whether Deep RL actor-critic methods could outperform the then state-of-the-art model (Pensieve) for server-side ABR.
        \item Adapted Adversary-Robust A3C (AR-A3C) to the discrete-action ABR setting and diagnosed why it failed. Rounding the convex combination of protagonist and adversary actions onto a discrete bitrates eliminates consistent adversarial pressure. A train–test distribution shift, caused by removing the adversary at evaluation, compounds the problem.
        \item When using PPO in the \href{https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3098822.3098843}{Pensieve} framework, combining advantage normalization with gradient clipping raised the average peak Quality of Experience (QoE) to 47.48 across 5 seeds, compared with 17.37 for baseline PPO. The resulting policy surpassed the Pensieve A3C learning curve within \(5 \times 10^{5}\) timesteps, whereas Pensieve required \(10^{6}\). Training throughput also rose by approximately \(21\%\) (2,100 vs. 1,730 timesteps/s).
        \item Ran a multi-seed ablation of five PPO implementation-level optimizations: value-function clipping, gradient clipping, KL-based early stopping, advantage normalization, and learning-rate annealing. The study isolated advantage normalization and gradient clipping as the only interventions that improved both convergence stability and peak QoE.
    \end{itemize}
